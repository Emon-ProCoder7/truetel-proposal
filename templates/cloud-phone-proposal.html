4. Cloud Phone System Solutions

A Complete UCaaS Platform

TrueTel's Cloud PBX is a complete Unified Communications as a Service (UCaaS) platform. It decouples your business voice from your physical office, allowing your team to operate seamlessly from the office, home, or on the road.

Unified Communications App

Empower your team with the TrueTel softphone app, available on iOS, Android, and desktop.

  • One Number Reach: Make and receive calls showing your business caller ID.
  • Visual Voicemail: Voicemail-to-email and in-app playback.
  • Team Presence: See who is available, busy, or on a call.
  • Work Anywhere: Staff can remain connected from home or on the road.
